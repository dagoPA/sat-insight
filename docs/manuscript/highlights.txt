Highlights (Remote Sensing of Environment)

- Municipal aggregates alone train maps scored on 61,430 held-out census tracts
- Weak supervision recovers 84-90% of the fully supervised upper bound
- Attention MIL maps are at chance; bag error barely predicts map quality
- Nighttime lights order tracts within municipalities as well as the trained map
- The learned deprivation gradient transfers zero-shot to Bogota block strata
